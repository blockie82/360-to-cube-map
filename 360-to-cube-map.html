360 to cube map

Drop in one or many equirectangular (2:1) 360 panoramas. Each becomes six square faces, ready to download per image or as a batch. Everything runs in your browser.

No images loaded.

Individual folders: one subfolder per image. Combined folder: every face in a single folder with unique names (name_px, name_nx…), which is what Reality Capture needs.

Face overlap widens each face beyond 90° so neighbouring faces share image content. This helps photogrammetry and Gaussian splatting match features across face borders. With overlap, the layout image is no longer a seamless cross, so use the individual faces for reconstruction. Each card lists the field of view and focal length to enter in your software.

Click any face to view it large and paint over things to leave out, such as people, a tripod or the sky. Masked areas are removed on export: either made transparent (masked faces are saved as PNG) or written as separate black-and-white mask files named name_px_mask.png (white keeps, black is ignored). Set the overlap before masking, as changing it clears masks.

Axis convention: the middle of each panorama becomes the front face (−Z), +Y up. Face names follow the OpenGL/Three.js order: px, nx, py, ny, pz, nz.

Paint over anything to exclude. Left and right arrow keys switch faces; Esc closes.
